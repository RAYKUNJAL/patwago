Solo traveler safety

Explore Jamaica with confidence

Built for solo female travelers and anyone exploring the island alone. Check in, share your location, get US State Department alerts, and trigger an SOS with one tap.

Solo Traveler Mode

One toggle turns on the full safety suite — automatic check-in reminders, last-known-location sharing with your trusted contacts, and a one-tap SOS button. Built around the advice real solo travelers share on Reddit and X.

STEP-aligned

Check-in Reminders

Set a daily check-in window. If you miss it, PatWaGo pings your emergency contacts automatically. Quick status updates — safe, concerned, or need help — keep loved ones in the loop.

Last Known Location

Every check-in records your GPS coordinates. Your trusted contacts can see where you last checked in — no battery-draining live tracking, just peace of mind on your terms.

One-Tap SOS Button

Hit the SOS button and PatWaGo logs an emergency event with your location and a custom message, then alerts every emergency contact flagged for SOS. Operators can acknowledge and resolve from the dashboard.

US State Department Alerts

Integrated with the Smart Traveler Enrollment Program (STEP) data model — receive embassy alerts and travel advisories for your area, just like US citizens abroad get from their nearest consulate.

Emergency Contacts

Add trusted people who get notified on missed check-ins and SOS events. Set which contacts hear about what — granular control over who is alerted in an emergency.

Community-Driven Alerts

Travelers and verified local vendors report real-time safety alerts — from pickpocketing hotspots to road conditions after rain. Geofiltered to show only what is near you.
